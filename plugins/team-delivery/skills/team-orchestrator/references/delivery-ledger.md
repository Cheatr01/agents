# Delivery ledger

Create this only for a multi-turn or multi-agent increment. Keep it under 30 lines and update it in place.

```markdown
# Delivery ledger — <increment>

Scope: <one line>
Non-goal: <one line>
Decision: <only active decision, or none>
Owners: <agent → files>
Evidence: focused=<status>; integration=<status>; build=<status>; manual=<status>
Risk: C=<n> R=<n> E=<n>; change=<none or reason>
Token budget: <measured|estimated> <limit>; spent=<value|unavailable>
Check history: <path or none>
Next: <one bounded action>
Blocked: <none or user decision>
```

Store links, log paths, and issue IDs—not pasted logs or full reports. Delete completed transient notes when a final handoff has captured the outcome.
